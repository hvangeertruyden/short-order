The loop

Server: take the order at the counter → ring it up (they get a number tent) → make their drink (soda: cup → ice → fill → lid; coffee: mug → brew) → deliver the tray to the table on the receipt → take payment → clear the table → drop dishes at the pass.

Cook: print tickets from the TV → grab the receipt → take ingredients from the fridge, freezer & bread box → cook them step by step (flip! stir! lift!) → finish at the prep counter → plate on the pass with the receipt → ring the bell → wash dishes.

Dirty plates make people sick. Noise means nobody can hear the server. Talk to each other.
